AIA — voice commands
==========================================================================

Wake word: 小艾同学

Say the wake word, then the command — either in one breath
("小艾同学播放五月天") or with a pause after it.

Phrases are matched by SOUND, not spelling, so they do not have to be
hit exactly: 不放歌曲 still reaches `resume`, and 下一手 reaches `next`.
Anything not recognised as a command is repeated back.

<...> marks a free-text argument. It always runs to the end of the
sentence, because speech has no reliable way to mark where it stops.

==========================================================================
CONFIRMATION
==========================================================================

Commands marked ASKS FOR CONFIRMATION say what they are about to do
and then keep listening — no wake word needed for the answer.

  yes : 确定 · 是 · 是的 · 对 · 好 · 好的 · 可以 · 没错 · yes · sure · ok
  no  : 取消 · 不 · 不要 · 别 · 算了 · 不用 · no · cancel · never mind

Silence, or anything that is neither, cancels. For something
irreversible, 'I could not tell' has to mean no.
